FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to the things business owners ask most. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a business website cost?

Our packages start at ₹4,999 for a Starter website, ₹9,999 for a Business website and ₹19,999 for a Premium website. These are starting prices — your final quotation depends on the pages, features and integrations you need.

How long does website development take?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly content such as text, logo and images is shared. A simple 1–3 page website can often be completed within a few days, while larger sites take longer. We confirm the timeline in your quotation before starting.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first and checked on different screen sizes, so it looks and works well on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. We add WhatsApp click-to-chat buttons with a prefilled message, so customers can reach you in one tap from any page.

Can I connect my own domain?

Yes. We can connect your own domain name to the website. If you do not have one yet, we can guide you on choosing and buying one.

Is hosting included in the price?

Domain and hosting are separate costs paid to the provider unless your quotation says otherwise. We will explain the options clearly and help you set everything up.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. You can ask us to make updates at any time, and for suitable projects we can add an easy content-management or admin area so you can edit content yourself.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance such as content updates, fixes and small improvements. Scope and pricing are agreed individually.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current website and redesign it with a modern look, better structure, mobile responsiveness and improved performance.

Can I request a free demo?

Yes. Fill in the demo request form or message us on WhatsApp with a few details about your business, and we will get back to you with a customised demo proposal.
